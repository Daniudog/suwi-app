Body doubling rooms
Work silently alongside others — proven to boost focus by up to 4×
Suwi explains body doubling
What is body doubling?
Body doubling is a focus technique where you work alongside another person — even silently. Research shows human presence (real or virtual) activates the brain's social accountability system, making it significantly harder to procrastinate.

How to use these rooms:
1. Pick a room that matches your task type
2. Type your intention — what you will work on
3. Click "Focus now" — your timer starts automatically
4. Work silently until your session ends

Why it works:
Even knowing others are working creates a sense of shared accountability. Studies show productivity increases by up to 4× with body doubling. It's used widely for ADHD, procrastination, and deep work.

Note: These rooms currently show active community members. Real-time multi-user rooms with live presence are coming in the next update.
